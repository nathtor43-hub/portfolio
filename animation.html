Animation

In this animation, I decided to portray a sense of innocent happiness using a very childlike style. This is evident in my character ”a baby basketball” and in the playful, endearing way it moves. I also added baby sounds to reinforce that feeling and make the animation feel more cheerful and fun. From the storyboard stage to the final result, I modified some of the movements because the ones I had originally planned proved too difficult to execute. I simplified them so they would work more effectively within After Effects. I utilized position, rotation, scale, and opacity properties to animate the character. The most challenging part was organizing the layers, as they would occasionally overlap and obscure one another; however, thanks to various tutorials, I learned how to arrange them more efficiently and was able to resolve the issue.
