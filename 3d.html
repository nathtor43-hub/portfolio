3D

My project consists of a 3D room inspired by a teenager's bedroom, designed as the setting for a suspense video game similar to “Five Nights at Freddy's”. I decided to construct only two walls so that the interior would be clearly visible, much like an open set used in video games or animation. I utilized simple geometric shapes in Tinkercad to create the furniture and maintain balanced proportions within the space. The most challenging part was arranging and resizing the objects to ensure the room appeared both realistic and functional for a video game environment.
