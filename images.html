Images

I chose to set my project in Central Park because it is a place of great significance to me; when I first arrived in this city, it was one of the very first places I wanted to visit, and I hold very special memories of my time there. For the composition, I combined a total of seven images, relying primarily on the resources provided by the professor on Brightspace as my reference. Upon comparing the techniques, I noticed that the "old school" method in Photoshop is far more time-consuming but yields a more precise result, whereas AI-assisted tools are faster and more efficient, albeit sometimes less accurate. For the AI-generated image, I used Copilot and was surprised by how closely the result matched my vision,a success I attribute largely to crafting a well-structured prompt. My biggest challenge involved removing the background from an image of people practicing yoga; I resolved this by adjusting the framing and minimizing the visibility of any imperfect details.
