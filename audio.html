Audio

In my audio project, I created a scene where two friends are heading to a concert. I combined eight audio samples, including several ambient crowd sounds, to recreate the busy and energetic environment of a concert audience. I also added effects like footsteps to make the characters’ movements more noticeable throughout the piece. The most challenging part was ensuring that no sound overpowered another, which I solved by carefully adjusting the volume levels of each track.
